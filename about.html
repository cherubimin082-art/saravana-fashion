About Us

Who We Are

Saravana Fashion is a Tiruppur-based readymade garments manufacturer, located on Dharapuram Road in Thillai Nagar. Tiruppur is known worldwide as India's knitwear and garment manufacturing hub, and we're proud to be part of that tradition.

We manufacture a wide range of clothing for the whole family — Men's Wear, Women's Wear and Kids' Wear — combining comfortable fabric with everyday style.

What We Do

From sourcing quality cotton and blended fabrics to stitching, finishing and packing, we manage the full garment-making process in-house, so every piece that leaves our facility meets a consistent standard of quality.
